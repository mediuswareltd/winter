# Guide

## What it is

Multimodal retrieval over speech and keyframes so every answer is cited by timestamp.

## Stack

`Python`, `Whisper`, `CLIP`, `Claude`

## Capabilities

### Speech + vision

Whisper transcripts plus CLIP keyframe search.

### Timestamp citations

Click a cite and the player seeks there.

### Hybrid retrieval

Dense + BM25 fused with RRF.

### Any video source

Upload files or paste YouTube URLs.
